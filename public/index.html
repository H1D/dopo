Welcome to dopo

dopo shows you your uncategorized Lunch Money transactions one at a time, so you can sort them with a swipe, a tap, or the keyboard instead of digging through a spreadsheet.

There's no dopo server. Your tokens stay on this device and go only to Lunch Money and OpenRouter — you'll choose your AI options on the next screens.

You'll need to be online for this first step. After that, dopo keeps working offline and catches up when you're back.

Connect Lunch Money

  1. Open your Lunch Money developer page.
  2. Select "Request new access token".
  3. Copy the token it shows you.

This token can see and change everything in your Lunch Money account — keep it as safe as a bank password, and don't share it.

Choose an AI helper

dopo can suggest a category for each transaction. Pick how you want that to work — you can always change this later in Settings.

How to get one:

  1. Sign up at openrouter.ai and add a few dollars of credit — that covers months of sorting.
  2. Open openrouter.ai/keys, choose "Create API key", and set a spend limit so the worst case is bounded.
  3. Copy the key and paste it above.

Here's how sorting works:

  • ✓ Swipe right or press → to sort it
  • 💤 Swipe left or press ← to save it for later
  • 🗂 Tap the card or press Enter to pick a category
  • ↺ Press U any time to undo
